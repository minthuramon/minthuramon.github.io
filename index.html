Min Thura Mon

PhD Candidate in Economics  ·  Macro-Labor

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at CMU, specializing in macroeconomics and labor economics. I study economic inequality and human capital formation, focusing on how households, firms and institutions shape economic opportunities and long-run development in emerging economies. My secondary research interests are in energy and environmental economics, examining how environmental shocks and the energy transition reshape labor markets, human capital, and firm dynamics.

Methodologically, I work with causal inference, causal machine learning, and structural modeling, including heterogeneous-agent, life-cycle and overlapping-generations models.

I am also an affiliate scholar at the Centre for the Study of Governance and Society at KCL. I hold graduate degrees in Economics and Environmental Management.
